# Resetting Your Company Password

Passwords expire every 90 days. You can reset yours without contacting IT.

1. Go to the self-service portal at password.example-corp.com.
2. Click "Forgot password" and enter your work email.
3. Confirm your identity with the code sent to your registered phone.
4. Choose a new password: at least 12 characters, with no reuse of your last 5 passwords.
5. Sign out and sign in again on all devices.

If you no longer have access to your registered phone, contact the service desk and bring a photo ID.
