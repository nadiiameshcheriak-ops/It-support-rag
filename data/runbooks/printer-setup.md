# Connecting to the Office Printer

Applies to: the shared printer "PRN-FLOOR2" on the second floor.

1. Open Settings > Printers & scanners and click "Add device".
2. Wait for "PRN-FLOOR2" to appear and select it.
3. If it does not appear, choose "Add manually" and enter the address \\print01\PRN-FLOOR2.
4. Print a test page to confirm.

If the printer shows "Offline", check that the laptop is on the office network (not guest Wi-Fi) and restart the Print Spooler service.
