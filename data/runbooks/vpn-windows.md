# VPN Setup on Windows

Applies to: company laptops running Windows 10 or 11.

1. Open Settings > Network & Internet > VPN.
2. Click "Add VPN" and choose "Windows (built-in)".
3. Set the connection name to "CorpVPN" and the server to vpn.example-corp.com.
4. Choose "Username and password" as the sign-in type.
5. Save, then click "Connect" and sign in with your company account.
6. Approve the second-factor prompt on your phone.

If the connection fails, check that you are not on a guest Wi-Fi network and restart the laptop.
