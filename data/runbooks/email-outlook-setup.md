# Setting Up Company Email in Outlook

Applies to: Outlook desktop app on Windows or macOS.

1. Open Outlook and click "Add Account".
2. Enter your work email address and click "Connect".
3. Sign in with your company account.
4. Approve the second-factor prompt on your phone.
5. Wait a few minutes while your mailbox syncs.

If Outlook keeps asking for a password, sign out of all Microsoft accounts in Outlook and try again.
