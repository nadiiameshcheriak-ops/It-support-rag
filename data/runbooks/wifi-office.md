# Connecting to Office Wi-Fi

Network name: CorpWiFi (for company laptops). Guests use "CorpGuest".

1. Open the Wi-Fi menu and select "CorpWiFi".
2. Sign in with your company username and password.
3. Accept the security certificate if asked.

Personal phones and tablets must use "CorpGuest". Company resources such as the printer and shared drive are not reachable from the guest network.
