# Lost or Stolen Laptop or Phone

Act quickly. Every hour matters.

1. Report the loss to the service desk immediately, even outside working hours (phone: 000-000-000).
2. Change your company password from another device.
3. IT will lock the device remotely and sign it out of all accounts.
4. If it was stolen, also file a report with the police and send the report number to IT.

Do not wait to see whether the device turns up.
